**An AI-native collaborative ledger for iPhone.** Snap a receipt or just say what happened — the agent turns it into structured expenses, splits, and balances for everyone on the trip.

- **Agent first** — no forms. Describe it, photograph it, correct it in conversation.
- **Receipt understanding** — line items, tax, fees, and discounts, split by item or evenly.
- **Shared ledgers** — invite friends, sync across devices, settle up in any currency.
- **Map** — every expense pinned where it happened.
- **Local first** — your ledger lives on your device; the cloud syncs, it doesn't own.

Built with UIKit + SwiftUI for iOS 26. Musubicho starts with the money side of a trip and is growing toward a full travel companion for planning, moving, and remembering together.

**AI 协同记账 / AA 分账 iPhone App。** 拍一张小票，或者直接说发生了什么，Agent 会把它整理成结构化的消费、分摊与结余。

- **Agent First**：不填表单，描述、拍照、在对话里修改即可
- **小票理解**：识别明细、税费与折扣，按项目或平均分摊
- **协同账本**：邀请同伴，多设备同步，支持多币种结算
- **地图**：每笔消费都标在发生的地方
- **Local First**：账本在你的设备上，云端只负责同步

Musubicho 从旅行中的「钱」开始，逐步走向一起计划、出行和回忆的完整旅行伙伴。
